# Changelog

## v0.2 — Vintage Hotel Management Foundation

### Added

- Persistent GameState autoload
- Ghost Coins
- Hotel reputation
- Multi-night progression
- Dynamic guest counts
- Room unlocks and upgrades
- 12-room haunted hotel catalogue
- Interactive three-floor hotel map
- Persistent room occupancy
- Ghost Book collection system
- 18 discoverable spirits
- Ghost rarity, personality, preferences, dislikes, and lore
- Night Ledger reward screen
- Random nightly hotel events
- Later-night guest unlocks
- VIP progression
- Special guest mechanics
- Legendary boss guest behavior
- Updated vintage haunted-hotel main menu
- Design direction documentation

### Special guests

- The Bell Twins
- Banshee Beatrice
- The Poltergeist
- Count Vesper
- Madame Umbra

### Gameplay progression

Night shifts now increase in difficulty through larger guest counts, shorter patience windows, new guest types, VIP arrivals, and random events.

### Visual direction

The interface has moved from the earlier purple arcade presentation toward an old Victorian hotel style with dark wood, brass accents, candlelight, aged parchment, and warm gothic presentation.
